PMID: 41672042
Title: Doping cDC1s for tolerance.

Abstract:
Type 1 conventional dendritic cells (cDC1s) are key antigen-presenting cells and have been implicated in the induction of peripheral tolerance, yet how cDC1s actively enforce immune tolerance remains unclear. In a recent issue of Nature, Zhang et al. propose that erythropoietin receptor signaling biases cDC1s toward a maturation state associated with immune tolerance across transplantation, homeostasis, and cancer.
