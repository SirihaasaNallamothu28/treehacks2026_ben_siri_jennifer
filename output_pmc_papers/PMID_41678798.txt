PMID: 41678798
Title: Remote Patient Monitoring Use Among Commercially Insured Adults With Cancer.
Journal: JMIR cancer
Authors: Joo, Joseph H; Jiang, Changchuan; Billig, Jessica I; Lieu, Nadia; Yang, Shining; Jin, Yujia; Hong, Arthur S; Liao, Joshua M

Abstract:
Our study describes the characteristics of remote patient monitoring use among commercially insured patients with cancer from 2019 to 2023.
