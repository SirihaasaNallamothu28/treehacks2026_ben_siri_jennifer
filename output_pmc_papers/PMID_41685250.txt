PMID: 41685250
Title: Leptin receptor rs1137101 polymorphism and altered leptin-sOb-R axis contribute to type 2 diabetes risk in Gujarat population.
Journal: Frontiers in endocrinology
Authors: Parmar, Nishant; Rathwa, Nirali; Patel, Roma; Pramanik Palit, Sayantani; Patel, Naisargi; Shetty, Satyashree; Dwivedi, Mitesh; Begum, Rasheedunnisa; Ramachandran, A V

Abstract:
Leptin (LEP), a pro-inflammatory adipokine secreted by adipocytes acting through leptin receptor (LEPR), is critical in maintaining body weight, lipid and glucose metabolism. <i>LEP</i> and <i>LEPR</i> genetic variants are reportedly associated with type 2 diabetes (T2D). Among these, the <i>LEPR rs1137101</i> A/G polymorphism has emerged as a potential determinant of metabolic risk. The current study investigates the association of <i>LEP</i> and <i>LEPR</i> genetic variants, along with their transcript and protein levels, and evaluates genotype-phenotype correlations with metabolic parameters and T2D susceptibility in the Gujarat population.
Genomic DNA isolated from PBMCs of 451 controls and 439 patients was used for genotyping <i>LEP</i> (<i>rs7799039</i> G/A; <i>rs2167270</i> G/A) and <i>LEPR</i> (<i>rs1137101</i> A/G; <i>rs1805094</i> G/C) polymorphisms by PCR-RFLP. RNA isolated from PBMCs was used to assess <i>LEP</i> and <i>LEPR</i> transcript levels by qPCR. Fasting Blood Glucose (FBG) levels, Body Mass Index (BMI) and plasma lipid profile were also assessed for the genotype-phenotype correlation analysis. ELISA was performed to estimate plasma protein levels of leptin and its soluble receptor (sOb-R).
Our findings suggest a significant association of <i>LEPR rs1137101 A/G</i> with T2D, where the GG genotype and G allele conferred a 1.66- and 1.24-fold increased risk for the disease, respectively. The GG genotype also showed an association with increased FBG and TC levels. In addition, an increased GG haplotype frequency, increased <i>LEP</i> transcript and protein levels, and decreased <i>LEPR</i> transcript and protein levels were observed in T2D patients. Moreover, leptin protein levels showed a positive correlation with increased BMI and TG, while sOb-R protein levels showed a positive correlation with increased BMI, FBG, and TG levels.
The <i>LEPR rs1137101 A/G</i> polymorphism, together with elevated leptin, and decreased sOb-R protein levels, may increase susceptibility to T2D in the Gujarat population.
