PMID: 41685732
Title: Robotic-assisted versus conventional total knee arthroplasty for treating osteoarthritis.
Journal: The Cochrane database of systematic reviews
Authors: Costa, Lauro Av; Dias, Celso Gp; Moraes, Vinícius Y; Antonioli, Eliane; Lenza, Mário

Abstract:
This is a protocol for a Cochrane Review (intervention). The objectives are as follows: To assess the benefits and harms of robotic-assisted versus conventional total knee arthroplasty for the treatment of osteoarthritis.
